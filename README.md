## About the Game

Released on **August 13, 2015**, *Criminal Case: World Edition* takes the investigation beyond local jurisdictions and brings the entire world into the picture.

Various law enforcement agencies govern different localities across the globe. However, the **Bureau** serves as the highest-level police force, specializing in complex and difficult crimes that ordinary local law enforcement agencies cannot handle.
